    Three simple steps三個簡單步驟

    If households who can prepare do, emergency services can focus on the people who need help most.有能力的家庭先做好準備，救援人員便可優先照顧最需要幫助的人。

    1

    Get your supplies準備物資

    • Water, food you normally eat, torch, radio, power bank食水、平日會吃的乾糧、電筒、收音機、充電寶
    • Add a few items each shop to spread the cost每次買餸加一兩件，分散開支
    • Check dates and refresh every 6 months每半年檢查有效日期並更換
    2

    Make a plan訂立計劃

    • Agree a meeting point and an out-of-area contact約定集合地點及外地聯絡人
    • Swap numbers with neighbours; ask who may need help同鄰居交換電話，了解誰需要幫忙
    • Check if you can join the free Priority Services Register看看能否登記免費的「優先服務名冊」(PSR)
    3

    Stay informed掌握資訊

    • Keep Emergency Alerts switched on on your phone手機保持開啟「緊急警報」
    • Sign up for Met Office and flood warnings訂閱氣象局及水浸警告
    • Keep a battery or wind-up radio for local news備用電池或手搖收音機收聽本地新聞

    Be digitally ready數碼備災

    Be Ready names cyber attacks alongside storms. When the internet, card payments or online services stop, these habits keep you going.「Be Ready」把網絡攻擊與風暴並列。當網絡、拍卡或網上服務停頓，以下習慣能讓你照常應付。

    Cash, not just cards現金，不只靠卡

    Card machines and banking apps need power and internet. Keep small notes and coins.拍卡機和銀行 App 都要電和網絡，家中留些散紙和硬幣。

    Paper copies紙本副本

    Print key phone numbers, medication names and doses, and ID copies. Keep them in a waterproof bag.打印重要電話、藥物名稱劑量及身份證明副本，放入防水袋。

    Charged and offline充好電・可離線

    Keep a full power bank, download offline maps of your area, and save 2-step backup codes on paper.充滿充電寶、下載本區離線地圖、把雙重認證備用碼抄在紙上。

    Spot the scams提防騙案

    Crises bring fake “refund” texts and calls. Official bodies won’t ask for your bank details by message.危機時常有假「退款」短訊和來電。官方機構不會用短訊索取銀行資料。
